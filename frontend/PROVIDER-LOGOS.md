# Payment provider logo sources

payment methods. Their trademarks remain with the respective providers; ZevaPay
does not claim affiliation or endorsement.

| File | Provider | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `bkash.svg` | bKash | [Logotyp.us bKash vector](https://logotyp.us/file/bkash.svg); third-party hosted vector artwork. |

The images are resized proportionally in the UI and are not recolored or
otherwise edited.
